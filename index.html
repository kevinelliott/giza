Achievements

    Giza Plateau — the real numbers

    • Great Pyramid (Khufu): base 230.4 m, original height 146.6 m, face slope 51°50′. ~2.3 million blocks.
    • Interior route: original entrance on the north face ~17 m up → Descending Passage → Ascending Passage → Grand Gallery (46.7 m long, 8.6 m high) → King’s Chamber. A branch leads to the Queen’s Chamber and another down to the Subterranean Chamber.
    • King’s Chamber: red granite, 10.47 × 5.23 m, 5.85 m high, with the granite sarcophagus.
    • Khafre: base 215.25 m, slope 53°; keeps its casing near the top.
    • Menkaure: base 102.2 m; lowest courses were red Aswan granite.
    • Great Sphinx: 73 m long, 20 m high, faces due east.
    • Monument spacing and orientation are taken from real GPS coordinates; passage cross-sections are slightly enlarged so you can walk them upright (the real ones are ~1.0–1.2 m and require crouching).
