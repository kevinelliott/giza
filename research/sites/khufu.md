# Great Pyramid of Khufu

## Done
- Base 230.4 m, original height 146.6 m (today 138.8 m, flat top), slope 51°50′ — standard survey values. Origin of the game grid.
- Interior route (entrance 17 m up the north face → descending passage → ascending passage → Grand Gallery 46.7 m → King's Chamber 10.47 × 5.23 × 5.85 m; Queen's Chamber; Subterranean Chamber): lengths and angles (26.5°) from published sections. **Passage cross-sections deliberately enlarged** for walking.
- Chevron blocks over the entrance; al-Ma'mun's tunnel mouth; boat pits from OSM outlines; mortuary temple 52.5 × 40 m with basalt floor.
