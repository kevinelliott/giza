## Still assumed (no figure found yet)
- **Depth of the lower apartments** below the second chamber: 5.3 m, scaled off the published section (Commons "Menkaure pyramid scheme.svg") and consistent with Vyse's ~15 m below the base. Vyse & Perring's plates would give it.
- First-chamber height (2.13 m), the riser of the six steps (0.2 m), the length of the horizontal run before the granite chamber (1.2 m), the slope of the ramp (taken as 26°).
- The ramp and lower passage are 1.25 m high so the player can crouch through; really 0.904 m (a crawl).
- Position of the chambers east–west relative to the pyramid's axis follows from the door being centred on the entrance passage (Petrie: entrance in the middle of the face).
