# Pyramid of Menkaure

## Done
- Base 102.2 × 104.6 m (102.2 used), height 65 m, slope 51°20′; centre 574.4 m W, 739.2 m S of Khufu (Petrie); base 11.8 m higher (DEM).
- Lower 16 courses of red Aswan granite casing (band to 12 m); the 1196 breach in the north face (above the entrance); three queens' pyramids, valley temple outline and quarry from OSM.
